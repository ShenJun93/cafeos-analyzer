# Insight specification v0.1

Each insight must carry: type, severity, metric, scope, current value, baseline value, delta, coverage, confidence and evidence.

Initial detectors: revenue change, transaction-count change, AOV change, store underperformance, daypart change, product/category mix shift, emerging product, falling product, discount spike, refund spike, repeat-rate change and at-risk regulars.

An anomaly is not a diagnosis. A valid statement may say that lower order volume explains most of a sales decline. It may not claim poor service, employee misconduct or competitor movement without evidence.
